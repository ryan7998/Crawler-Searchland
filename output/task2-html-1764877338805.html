Building Notices - These are Notices served on the Council stating that building work is planned to be undertaken. The Council does not issue an Approval Notice for such works. If the work has commenced this will be shown on the individual records. A Completion Certificate can be purchased if the works are shown to have been completed. If works have commenced and a completion inspection date is not listed, there may be outstanding works, or notices that the owner/future owner of the property is responsible for. Please email buildingcontrol@westnorthants.gov.uk to request copies quoting application reference.

Full Plans - If a completed or decision date is shown a copy of the approval and/or completion certificate may be available to purchase from the Council. Please email buildingcontrol@westnorthants.gov.uk to request copies quoting application reference. Please note if works have commenced and a completed date is not listed, there may be outstanding works or notices that the owner/future owner of the property is responsible for.

Initial Notices - These are Notices served on the Council by a Private Building Control Body (Approved Inspector). It is a notice to inform the Council that a private company will be carrying out the building control function on a development. The approved inspector is responsible for the issue of the approval notice and completion certificate not Local Authority Building Control. Insurer details may not be present for all Approved Inspector applications. Please contact the Council if you require this information.

Competent Persons Self-Certification Notifications - Certain Building Regulation works can be undertaken by Competent Persons. On completion of their specific activity the Council receives an electronic notification and this information is contained in the register. The certificate of compliance is issued by the competent person not Local Authority Building Control. Further information about competent persons can be found at Competent person schemes.
